Boundary conventions (9): decide how these recurring cases should be tagged
When a part asks why a substance has a higher boiling point (or other property) because of a specific intermolecular force, is that force (e.g. hydrogen-bonding) co-primary with imf-properties, or supporting? Cases: mcq-2002-67-765 (stem), mcq-2022-21-1622 (stem)
AI suggestions (not decisions)Claude: A. The adjudicator chose A in every benchmark case and prompt v2 already states it; two primaries should mean two equally assessed ideas.
GPT-6 (independent AI review, not a decision): other (When a question primarily asks to compare or explain a physical property (boiling point, melting point, viscosity), tag imf-properties primary and the specific force supporting. When it directly asks which intermolecular attractions are broken or present, tag the specific force primary; do not add imf-properties merely because a phase change appears.). Boiling-point comparisons genuinely assess the macroscopic-property explanation, but the 2002 vaporization question asks specifically to identify hydrogen bonds; requiring imf-properties primary there would obscure the actual target.
When one part asks for the molecular shape and whether the molecule is polar, which is primary? Cases: frq-1999-7-564 (d)
AI suggestions (not decisions)Claude: C when the part asks for both answers explicitly; otherwise the one the part asks for is primary.
GPT-6 (independent AI review, not a decision): C (When shape and polarity are separately requested, make both primary; where Lewis-structure construction is also separately requested, do not silently demote the third assessed target solely due to a two-primary schema cap. Extend the schema or represent distinct rubric objectives.). The selenium FRQ separately asks for a Lewis drawing, shape sketch, and polarity judgment. Geometry is not merely an invisible step when it is an explicit deliverable.
For 'why does rate increase with temperature' questions, is the primary concept the collision model or activation energy (fraction of collisions above Ea)? Cases: mcq-2008-11-31 (stem)
AI suggestions (not decisions)Claude: C. It ties the tag to a visible feature, which makes it reproducible.
GPT-6 (independent AI review, not a decision): other (Qualitative explanations of increased collision frequency/effectiveness on changing temperature: collision-model primary, activation-energy supporting only if a threshold-energy fraction or distribution is explicitly required. If the stem directly asks what quantity is inferred from rates at several temperatures, activation-energy is primary; collision-model is not automatically supporting.). MCQ 2008 Q11 is a matching-list question asking which measurable quantity is obtained from temperature-dependent rates, not why increasing temperature speeds up the reaction.
For heating-curve or phase-change questions that ask whether a step absorbs or releases energy, is the primary concept phase-change energy or endothermic/exothermic processes? Cases: mcq-2008-13-32 (stem)
AI suggestions (not decisions)Claude: A. The CED places heating curves under phase-change energy; endo/exo is the general idea.
GPT-6 (independent AI review, not a decision): A. A direct phase-change enthalpy or heating-curve question should be searchable primarily under phase-change energy; endothermic/exothermic is often a sign interpretation. If ΔS is also separately tested, entropy may independently be primary.
When a part asks which species predominates in a buffer at a given pH (no calculation of pH), is the primary concept pKa/speciation or Henderson-Hasselbalch? Cases: frq-2003-3-536 (e)
AI suggestions (not decisions)Claude: A. It separates a qualitative comparison of pH with pKa from a calculation.
GPT-6 (independent AI review, not a decision): A. Comparing pH with pKa to infer predominant forms or equal conjugate amounts is primarily speciation. Require an actual use of the Henderson–Hasselbalch equation to compute pH or a non-obvious ratio before giving that as primary.
In a pH or acid-comparison question, does recognizing whether an acid is strong or weak earn a supporting strong-weak-acids tag? Cases: mcq-2016-9-1056 (stem), mcq-2022-52-1652 (stem)
AI suggestions (not decisions)Claude: B. Otherwise almost every acid-base question carries the tag and it stops being useful for search.
GPT-6 (independent AI review, not a decision): B. When an acid is already given as weak, recognizing that adjective is not an independent assessed skill. A strong/weak tag is useful when identifying acid type from a titration curve or comparing complete versus partial ionization is an explicit discriminating objective.
Does a question that shows a particle diagram of a solution earn a supporting solution-representations tag? Cases: mcq-2014-15-178 (stem), mcq-2022-56-1653 (stem), frq-2003-3-536 (e)
AI suggestions (not decisions)Claude: B. The representations field already captures the diagram and is searchable separately.
GPT-6 (independent AI review, not a decision): B. Reading any diagram should not automatically create a chemistry concept tag. Use the representations metadata for the medium; tag solution-representations as a concept when selecting, constructing, or assessing the adequacy of a particle depiction is itself an explicit task.
When a part solves a relationship for a quantity that has its own concept (ΔS° from ΔG° = ΔH° − TΔS°, ΔG° from −nFE°, a rate law from a 1/[A] plot, bond strength from bond order), which concept is primary? Cases: frq-2011-3-933 (c-ii), frq-2011-3-946 (c-ii), frq-2008-3-59 (b), mcq-2018-13-1198 (stem), mcq-2016-5-1053 (stem)
AI suggestions (not decisions)Claude: A. It matches how the CED groups equations (ΔG° = −nFE° is in 9.9, linear plots in 5.3) and keeps both concepts searchable. GPT-6 consistently chose B; Claude mostly chose C and so omitted entropy where ΔS° is the requested value.
For legacy 'write a balanced/net ionic equation for each reaction' items (pre-2014 FRQ format), which concept is primary? Cases: frq-2005-4-10 (b), frq-2005-4-10 (c), frq-2005-4-10 (e), frq-2005-4-10 (f), frq-2005-4-10 (g)
AI suggestions (not decisions)Claude: A. Otherwise searches for net ionic equations fill up with retired-format prediction items, and the beyond-AP marker would sit on a supporting tag.
